ABOUT

Nice to meet you

Hi. My name is Amaris and I created this project during the Summer Immersion Program at Girls Who Code. I was motivated to make this website because playing video game is part of a daily routine I have, and my experience in playing certain games like Rocket League or Apex Legends I have went through my fair share of toxicity. So with this website, I was hoping to inform people about those spaces and how they can be harmful.
