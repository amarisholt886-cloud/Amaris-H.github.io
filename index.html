TOXIC GAMING SPACES

Why can gaming culture be so toxic? Especially in certain games such as:

COD, Counter Strike, and League of Legends are some of the most toxic..

Why are these games so toxic?

In my own opinion, since games like Call of Duty, Counter Strike and League of Legends are also very competitve, when people lose they tend to take it alot more seriously and lash out harsher. Also, because games are online, people might tend to feel they can say whatever they want, because there will be know consequences (besides maybe a minor ban) and no one knows who they are in real life.
