TAKE AN OATH

How can we solve toxic gaming spaces in all games?

The Truth

Unfotunatley eliminating all toxicity in gaming is not very possible, kind of like trying to eliminating all crime in the world. Yes there are rules in place to prevent it form happening, but people still break them. However there are ways that we can minimize it:


  • If you encounter a toxic gamer, simply mute or block them. Completely disengage and dont entertian their antics.
  • Make sure you report them so they atleast get some kind of punishment for their harrasment.
  • Remember, its just a game! Anyone crashing out like that over a video game needs some serious self reflection, meanwhile we can stay mature and positive. Your free time is meant for fun not stress. If you want you could even only play with friends to prevent random toxic teammates, although opponents can be toxic aswell so you might not be able to escape it.

An Oath

Gaming toxicity can have a serious effect of peoples mental health and stick with them forever. So below, you can take an optional oath, to promise you will never participate in the harrasment or hate of other people online (unless for self defense...maybe).



Below you can type in your name, and that you swear to never engage in any harrasment to another player. Yes people have bad days but dont take it out on innocent people! Get off the game and take a chill pill.
